Flagship publications

Each summary distinguishes the research question, evidence, method, contribution and boundary of the result. The figures are author-provided visuals from the studies.

01 · Safety Science · 2023 · First author

Predicting fatal fall from heights accidents using random forest classification machine-learning model

Evidence and method: seven classification methods were tested on 3,321 structured accident scenarios covering site, management, individual and agent-related factors.

Contribution: demonstrated a practical route from administrative fatal-accident records to an interpretable predictive model for construction-safety analysis.

Boundary: feature importance supports screening and prioritisation; it does not prove causation or replace incident investigation.

02 · Safety Science · 2022 · First author

Risk assessment of fatal accidents due to work-at-heights activities using fault tree analysis

Evidence and method: 128 fatal cases were coded into contributing factors, immediate causes and root causes, then reconstructed as fault-tree pathways.

Contribution: moved beyond a list of causes by showing how management, work-process and immediate-event failures combine within fatal-event pathways.

Main priorities: inadequate supervision and leadership, failure to use PPE, and absent or poorly followed work standards.

Detailed paper summary →
03 · Fire Technology · 2026 · First author

Linking Accident Data and Physics-Based Modeling for Hydrogen Fire Safety

Evidence and method: 954 hydrogen-related incidents were consolidated and translated into screening-level descriptors using first-order engineering relationships and Monte Carlo uncertainty propagation.

Contribution: provides a transparent bridge between accident databases and engineering interpretation without treating reconstructed quantities as direct measurements.

Boundary: the outputs support comparative screening; they are not detailed consequence-model validation.

Supporting visual studies

These papers broaden the programme across accident patterns, environmental health, intelligent manufacturing and sustainability methods.

Journal of Safety Research · 2023

Investigating patterns of workplace fatal fall injuries

Examined occupations, causes, contributing factors and time patterns across 3,321 scenarios. General workers were the most exposed group, with roofers and electricians also prominent.

Environmental health · 2024

Health risk from coal-fired power-plant emissions

Combined emissions and dispersion assessment to identify pollutants of concern and differences in chronic and acute risk between population groups.

Journal of Electrical Engineering · 2024

Forecasting material quantity using machine learning and time-series methods

Compared tree-based, regression, neural-network and recurrent models for material forecasting in cement production.

Chemical Engineering Transactions · 2025

Life-cycle assessment methods for paper-based products

Reviewed process-based, input–output and hybrid LCA approaches, focusing on how methodological choices affect coverage, data requirements and interpretation.
