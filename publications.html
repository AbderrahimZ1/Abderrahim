Publications by Dr Abderrahim Zermane

Selected findings

The strongest contribution of this body of work is its movement from descriptive accident counts toward explicit causal pathways and interpretable predictive models.

Work at heightSupervision and system controls emerged as urgent priorities

In a fault-tree study of Malaysian fatal falls, lack of supervision and leadership, failure to use PPE, and absent or poorly followed work standards were among the highest-occurrence causes.

Incident patterns3,321 fatal-fall scenarios were structured and analysed

General workers were the most vulnerable occupational group in the dataset, while roofers and electricians also showed prominent annual fatality shares.

Explainable machine learningSeven classification approaches were tested

Random forest was selected to model probable factors affecting fatal falls and to compare the predictive contribution of site, management, individual and agent factors.
