01 · Safety Science · 2023 · First author

Predicting fatal fall from heights accidents using random forest classification machine-learning model

My primary flagship publication. Seven models were tested using 3,321 accident scenarios. Random Forest was selected for its predictive performance and ability to reveal the contribution of site, management, individual and agent factors.

  • 3,321 structured accident scenarios
  • Seven classification models evaluated
  • Explainable factor-level prediction

02 · Safety Science · 2022 · First author

Risk assessment of fatal accidents due to work-at-heights activities using fault tree analysis

Accident pathways were reconstructed from 128 fatal cases, moving beyond descriptive counts to show how contributing factors, immediate causes and root causes combine.

Linking Accident Data and Physics-Based Modeling for Hydrogen Fire Safety
