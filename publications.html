Flagship publications

The images are taken from the papers themselves. Each summary distinguishes what the study did from what the results can reasonably support.

01 · Safety Science · 2023 · First author

Predicting fatal fall from heights accidents using random forest classification machine-learning model

Seven classification methods were tested on 3,321 structured accident scenarios. Random Forest was selected for its performance and its usefulness in examining how recorded site, management, individual and agent factors contributed to predictions.

Contribution: demonstrated a practical route from administrative fatal-accident records to an interpretable predictive model for construction-safety analysis.

02 · Safety Science · 2022 · First author

Risk assessment of fatal accidents due to work-at-heights activities using fault tree analysis

The study reconstructed pathways from 128 fatal cases. The highest-occurrence priorities included inadequate supervision and leadership, failure to use PPE, and absent or poorly followed work standards.

Contribution: moved beyond a list of causes by showing how contributing factors, immediate causes and root causes combine within fatal-event pathways.

Detailed paper summary →
03 · Fire Technology · 2026 · First author

Linking Accident Data and Physics-Based Modeling for Hydrogen Fire Safety

The paper consolidates 954 hydrogen-related incidents and reconstructs screening-level physical descriptors, including fuel mass, heat-release rate, flame length and explosion-energy potential. Monte Carlo analysis carries uncertainty from incomplete records into the estimates.

Contribution: provides a transparent bridge between accident databases and engineering interpretation without treating reconstructed quantities as direct measurements.

Journal of Safety Research · 2023

Investigating patterns of workplace fatal fall injuries

Examined occupations, causes, contributing factors and time patterns across 3,321 scenarios. General workers were the most exposed group, with roofers and electricians also prominent.

Health risk from coal-fired power-plant emissions

Forecasting material quantity using machine learning and time-series methods

Chemical Engineering Transactions · 2025

Life-cycle assessment methods for paper-based products

Reviewed process-based, input–output and hybrid LCA approaches, focusing on how methodological choices affect coverage, data requirements and interpretation.
