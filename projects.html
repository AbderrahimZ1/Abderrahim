Urban fire and multi-hazard modelling

The fire-modelling projects connect hazard physics to exposure, fragility, uncertainty and the decisions made by planners, engineers and risk stakeholders.

University College London · 2023–2025

Tomorrow's Cities: urban fire modelling

As a Research Fellow in Physics-based Fire Modelling at UCL, I co-developed an urban fire-spread model and integrated it into a wider multi-hazard and fragility decision framework. The contribution included tenability and threshold reasoning, validation and versioning discipline, and example-first materials that made the model easier to interpret within scenario planning.

City-scale Fire Spread Model

The original class-city / city-scale fire-safety model in GitHub represents the spread of fire through an urban building stock. It was developed to compare scenarios, explore how local fire behaviour can accumulate into city-scale consequences, and provide a base for later open and uncertainty-aware workflows.

The urban-fire work includes explicit scientific cautions: comparison templates are not presented as external validation until real experimental, FDS or incident-reference outputs are inserted. Environmental and safety decision systems

These tools apply the same research principle in a different form: every conclusion should remain connected to its evidence, assumptions and reviewer judgement.

Environmental review · Malaysia

SEED EIA

SEED EIA is an invite-only workspace for reviewing Environmental Impact Assessment reports against a workbook-aligned framework. The system organises 158 findings across nine review tabs and retains evidence excerpts, page references, confidence, assessment rationale, officer corrections and a complete audit trail. It distinguishes presence checks, cross-checks, qualitative review, Annex 5 criteria, digital authenticity and requirements that still need manual review.

Construction safety · Malaysia

Design Safety MY

A design-safety assurance platform structured around Malaysia's Occupational Safety and Health (Construction Work) (Design and Management) Regulations 2024. The system maps Regulations 1–39 to accountable actions and connects prevention-through-design decisions, DOSH-style HIRARC, bowtie assurance, controlled project records, revision intelligence, BCF issue exchange and regulatory-change impact assessment.

How the projects connect

EvidenceEvery finding has a source

InterpretationHuman judgement stays visible
