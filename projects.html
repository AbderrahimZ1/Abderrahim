Projects and research software

Systems that make assumptions, evidence and decisions visible.

My project work extends the research papers into reproducible modelling frameworks and review tools.

01
University College London · 2023–2025

Tomorrow’s Cities — Urban fire modelling

Co-developed a physics-based urban fire-spread model and integrated it into a multi-hazard and fragility decision workflow. The contribution covered fire behaviour, tenability thresholds, scenario validation, versioning and decision-ready communication.

  • City-scale exposure and spread
  • Multi-hazard integration
  • Tenability and threshold reasoning
  • Research and teaching artefacts
02
Open research framework

Open Urban Fire Framework

A reproducible scenario workflow connecting building footprints, inferred compartments, fuel loads, reduced fire physics, validation templates, district case studies and probabilistic uncertainty maps.

  • GeoJSON building import
  • Formal V&V reports
  • FDS-reference workflows
  • Reproducibility manifests
03
Environmental review

SEED EIA

An evidence-led review workspace for Malaysian Environmental Impact Assessment reports. The system preserves page references, confidence, assessment rationale, officer overrides and audit trails across 158 structured findings.

  • 9 workbook-aligned review areas
  • Page-aware text and visual evidence
  • Officer correction and auditability
  • Scanned-page and map handling
04
Construction safety · Malaysia

Design Safety MY

A prevention-through-design assurance platform aligned with Malaysia’s Construction Work (Design and Management) Regulations 2024, connecting duties, design risks, HIRARC, critical controls, evidence and revision intelligence.

05
Compliance architecture

OSHKKP Malaysia Cloud and Amane QHSE Cloud

Separate Malaysia- and Algeria-focused foundations for legal applicability, obligations, incidents, permits, inspections, competence, environmental compliance and management reporting.
