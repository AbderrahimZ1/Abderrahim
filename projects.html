Research appointmentUCL · 2023–2025

Tomorrow’s Cities: urban fire modelling

As a Research Fellow in Physics-based Fire Modelling, I contributed to an urban fire-spread model within a wider multi-hazard and fragility decision framework. The work addressed local fire behaviour, exposure, tenability thresholds, validation discipline, versioning and outputs that could be interpreted within comparative city scenarios.

Urban fire spreadMulti-hazardTenabilityScenario planning
Boundary: a comparative city-scenario model is not a substitute for project-specific fire engineering or high-fidelity analysis of every building.

Open Urban Fire Framework

A reproducible exposure-to-fire workflow connecting building footprints, inferred compartments and fire attributes, a reduced physics kernel, verification templates, district scenarios and probabilistic outputs. The framework makes evidence confidence and validation status part of the model object.

GeoJSONReduced fire physicsV&VUncertainty mapsReproducibility
Boundary: deterministic design use requires project-specific calibration and external validation. A coloured building map is not automatically a validated risk prediction.

Private working prototypeEnvironmental review · Malaysia

SEED EIA

An evidence-led workspace for reviewing Malaysian Environmental Impact Assessment reports. The system organises 158 findings across nine review areas and retains evidence excerpts, page references, confidence, rationale, officer corrections and audit history.

158 findingsPage provenanceMaps & tablesOfficer correctionAudit record
Boundary: SEED supports review; it does not make approval, legal or statutory decisions. The officer remains responsible for confirming evidence and recording the reviewed outcome.

Private developmentConstruction safety · Malaysia

Design Safety MY

A prevention-through-design assurance platform aligned with Malaysia’s Occupational Safety and Health (Construction Work) (Design and Management) Regulations 2024. It connects accountable duties, design decisions, HIRARC, critical controls, evidence and controlled deliverables.

CDM 2024Design riskHIRARCCritical controlsControlled evidence
Boundary: the platform structures duty and evidence workflows; it does not determine legal compliance without competent professional review.
