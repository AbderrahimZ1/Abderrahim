Open research workflow

Open Urban Fire Framework

A reproducible workflow connecting building footprints, inferred compartments, fuel-load assumptions, reduced fire physics, validation templates, district cases and probabilistic burn or ignition maps. The framework separates a model result from external validation and records what evidence is still missing.

Environmental and safety decision systems

These systems address a different technical problem: review findings often lose their connection to the source evidence and automated outputs can appear more certain than the underlying documents allow.

Environmental review · Malaysia

SEED EIA

An evidence-led workspace for reviewing Malaysian Environmental Impact Assessment reports. The system organises 158 findings across nine review areas and retains evidence excerpts, page references, confidence, rationale, officer corrections and audit history. Visual pages, maps and scans are handled separately from language-model interpretation rather than being treated as ordinary text.

How the projects are evaluated

EvidenceCan the conclusion be traced?

Page references, model inputs, official sources and reconstruction assumptions remain available for review.

UncertaintyIs the limit of the result visible?

Unsupported fields remain blank or qualified; reconstructed values are not presented as measurements.

UsefulnessDoes the output support a real action?

The intended result is a scenario, finding, control record or decision aid—not an impressive interface without an operational purpose.
